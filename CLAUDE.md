# Купи имот с Ник (n-kapitanov.github.io/properties)

Client page for Nik Kapitanov's properties. `index.html` holds everything; the property list is `const P=[...]`, one entry per property, photos in `images/NNN/01.jpg, 02.jpg, ...` (01 is the cover).

## Share links (link previews)
- Every property has a share page at `NNN/index.html`, so the link to send is `https://n-kapitanov.github.io/properties/NNN/`. It shows the property's cover photo, title and price in Viber/WhatsApp/Facebook previews and forwards the visitor to `../#pNNN`.
- Links with `#pNNN` show only the site logo (preview services ignore the part after `#`). Always give Nik the `/NNN/` form.
- The pages and the 1200x630 preview images (`images/NNN/og.jpg`) are generated by `tools/build_share_pages.py`. After adding, removing or changing a property (price, title, cover photo), run `python3 tools/build_share_pages.py` and commit the result. The GitHub Action `.github/workflows/share-pages.yml` also runs it on every push, as a safety net.
- Do not edit `NNN/index.html` or `og.jpg` by hand.
